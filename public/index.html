How it works

One shared plan, with fewer unanswered questions.

1

Guests add their needs

Each guest privately records the food allergens they need to avoid.

2

Everyone adds dishes

Contributors provide ingredients, allergen statements, and preparation information.

3

The menu is checked

TableForAll finds conflicts, missing details, and guests who may need additional choices.

What makes it different

Check the whole table, not one product at a time.

TableForAll looks across every guest and every dish. It can recommend the smallest changes needed to improve menu coverage while keeping unclear information visible.

Important allergy notice

TableForAll is a planning and communication tool. It does not certify that food is safe. Guests should confirm ingredients, preparation methods, and cross-contact concerns directly with the person preparing the food.
